If You’re Interested In Learning Android Development, Subscribe To This YouTube Channel

Aida IssayevaAugust 17, 20241 min read
PART OF AN EXPERIMENTWriting in public · Ship 30 for 30↗

Learning Android Development is hard.

Luckily, everything you need to know to get started, become proficient, and even get hired as an android developer, you have access to online—for free.

Just subscribe to these YouTube channel:

Android Developers Youtube Channel

The Android Developers YouTube Channel is the official platform for anyone interested in learning about Android development.

This channel provides the latest Android news, keeping developers informed about new features and updates in the ecosystem. It also offers best practices and tutorials, which are essential for mastering development techniques and improving coding skills. Live videos and demonstrations provide practical, real-time examples of how to solve common problems and implement new technologies in Android apps.

Subscribing to this channel is invaluable for both new and experienced developers looking to stay at the forefront of Android technology and continuously enhance their skills.

So, save yourself $15,000 studying android development in college/bootcamp and just binge this YouTube channel.
