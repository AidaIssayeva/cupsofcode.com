The One Software App I Can’t Live Without For Handling My Life

Aida IssayevaJuly 24, 20241 min read
PART OF AN EXPERIMENTWriting in public · Ship 30 for 30↗

Today, there is an app, website, or software platform for just about anything (including finding someone to walk your dog).

But when it comes to managing my life, there is one app I can’t live without:

Notion

And here’s why: Reason #1: It has seamless integration with many tools, making it a single source of truth for all action items across different tools such as emails, calendars, documents, and other project tracking apps.

Reason #2: It’s highly customizable, so I can set it up to fit my needs, whether it’s tracking positive habits on a daily basis or highlighting focus projects for the week.

Reason #3: It offers collaborative features, which makes it easy to work with others and keep everyone on the same page.

If you want to better organize your life in one place, remove clutter from your brain, or systemize and achieve your goals, I can’t recommend this app enough.
