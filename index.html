Limited time has made me less patient with business models that only work when the founder is constantly present. If every sale requires a call, every customer needs manual setup, and every week needs new content just to stay visible, I have not built freedom. I have built another job with worse benefits. Repost if this is the kind of business you are trying to avoid.

As a Staff Engineer, I planned around team capacity. As a solo founder and mother, I have to plan around interruption capacity too. Those are not the same thing.

Leaving employment gave me more control over my day. My 18-month-old immediately claimed most of it. My business has to survive daycare schedules, sick days, interrupted mornings, and work completed in fragments. That is not a temporary inconvenience to optimize away. It is part of the business model I chose. Follow if you are also building a business that has to fit a life, not consume it.

A feature request is not a command. It is evidence that someone’s real workflow stopped matching your product. A Blurts customer wanted her week to begin on a day other than Sunday or Monday. The useful question was not “Can I build this?” Of course I could. The useful question was “Does this belong in the product?” What filter do you use before turning a request into a feature?
