How the estimate works

Current PRs per week = engineers × pre-agent PRs × output multiplier. Weekly friction hours = current PRs × [(CI minutes × blocked percentage) + (retry-affected percentage × extra active recovery minutes)] ÷ 60. Capacity cost = annual friction hours × loaded hourly cost. Retry-affected PRs include automatic reruns and manual restarts even when the CI system hides them behind one final status.

Annual agent tokens = current PRs × AI-assisted share × tokens per assisted PR × working weeks. Model spend = annual tokens × blended cost per million. Tokens and model spend show the investment entering the pipeline; they are not assumed to be lost. Use total CI feedback time per PR and count only additional active troubleshooting in recovery to avoid double counting.

07 / BEFORE WE TALK

A few practical
questions.

Is this an audit of our AI coding tools?

No. The focus is the delivery system that receives AI-assisted output: builds, tests, CI, quality gates, review, merge, and release. Agent usage and PR volume establish the demand that system now has to absorb.

Why hire you instead of asking Claude or ChatGPT?

Use them - I do. AI can suggest fixes when you describe a known problem. The difficult part is determining which constraint is actually limiting your delivery system, measuring its cost, and choosing changes that improve throughput without lowering the quality bar. That requires your team’s delivery data, engineer interviews, and experienced technical judgment. You hire me for organization-specific diagnosis, Staff-level judgment, and an accountable roadmap - not a generic list of optimization tips.

Is this just a Gradle audit?

No. Builds are one part of the investigation. The audit follows the path from writing code through CI, review, merge, and release to find the bottlenecks that matter most.

How do you protect confidential information?

We agree on confidentiality, data handling, storage, and deletion expectations before any sensitive material is shared. I request the minimum practical access and prefer read-only access wherever it is sufficient.

Will you implement the fixes?

The audit delivers analysis and an actionable roadmap. If you want help executing it, we can scope a separate implementation sprint after reviewing the findings.

Do you guarantee a specific improvement?

No. The audit identifies opportunities using your team’s data. Results depend on the bottlenecks found, the changes you choose to make, and their implementation.
